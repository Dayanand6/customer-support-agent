# Demo Store support FAQ

This sample is fictional. Replace it with the company's approved policies before using the assistant with customers.

## Returns

Sealed, unused items may be returned within 30 days of delivery. A receipt or order confirmation is required. Personalized items and gift cards cannot be returned. Refunds are sent to the original payment method after the returned item is inspected.

## Exchanges

Size exchanges are available within 14 days of delivery when the item is unused and in its original packaging. Customers should contact the support team before mailing an exchange.

## Shipping

Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. This is an estimate, not a delivery guarantee. The support team can help locate an order when the customer provides an order number through the company's approved support channel.

## Contact

For an exception, damaged item, missing order, or any question not answered here, a human support teammate must review the case. The assistant cannot view orders or approve refunds.
